I
The empire

Anarchy at Samarra

The glorious Haqq-Caliphate Empire of Abbasiyya stretches from the Misrata system in the west to the Indumultan system in the east, a range spanning 53 light-years from end to end of a nebula. The haqq-caliphate has enjoyed its long-lasting Golden Age. Yet, the current haqq-caliph Mutawakkil is a tyrant emperor who throws centuries of progress all to the ground.

The nobles of the empire enjoy using another human beings as their playthings and pawns. They own slaves from their conquered lands. You are ghilman, the slave-soldiers/mercenaries of the empire’s armies. You were taken, abducted, and sold to the highest bidder. You, who were conquered from other lands, will conquer lands for the haqq-caliph.

However, ghilman is not just mere slaves. They can enjoy high pay, educated in various fields, access rare technology, and much more. They have power and political prowess. They can even buy their own freedom. In theory, anyways.

Realizing that Mutawakkil is a threat to his own empire, a group of ghilman murders him in public. Anarchy spreads to the rest of the Abbasid haqq-caliphate empire. Now, let the show begin: it’s time for the ghilman class, you, to rule!

II
Player characters

Ghilman Lancers

PCs are ghilman, slave-soldiers cavalry Lancers of the empire. The party belongs to a ghilman faction. During session 0, we will create the details of your ghilman faction and decide the faction’s head that will become leader, patron, and mentor to you. We will also decide your faction’s enemies, from the nobles side and the ghilman side.

This campaign focuses on the struggle for power to determine the next haqq-caliph; from the political intrigue of giving and taking favors and influences to military conflicts where factions collide with each other in sparks and blood. We will mostly roleplay as pilots. Expect big mecha combat to happen once every 2-3 roleplay sessions.

“Ghilman” is the plural form of the word, the singular form is “ghulam”. The word means boys or servants. Imagine a corps of elegant, youthful, androgynous cavalry lancers in the service of a galactic empire (yes, twink femboy Lancers). Ghilman are always the minority of the population. They are not united yet. Various factions fight one another for power. The head of a ghilman faction is usually the most powerful and/or the most cunning of the division who has great visions for Abbasiyya’s future.

III
Succession crisis

Factional power

Due to the nature of the haqq-caliphate, we can’t go full commie route here. Instead, each ghilman and nobles faction will pick a candidate that will become the next haqq-caliph in hope of stopping the anarchy. That’s why fellow ghilman factions just can’t get along.

“Nobles” refers to the aristocratic class that backed and benefited the old rule of Mutawakkil. They are mostly corrupt and have no respect and will for better progress. They are the last stands of Mutawakkil’s shadows who will clutch hard at their seats of powers. Just like the ghilman, the nobles are also disparate and not united.

IV
Capital planet

Samarra

The capital planet of Abbasiyya is Samarra, a lush metropolis full of tall buildings (either beautiful skyscraping apartments to the disgusting slum towers). The planet houses the decadent palace of the haqq-caliph, that becomes even more extravagant because of Mutawakkil’s spendings. This campaign will mostly set in Samarra, but if the needs arise, there is a possibility to go to other planets or frontiers of the haqq-caliphate, either for politicking or warring.

The haqq-caliphate exists in a nebula called Lalupa Capitolina. It is a nebula full of rare minerals, lush lands, space clouds prime to be researched, and other wonders. It is no surprise that this nebula is envied by the spacefaring civilizations that gaze upon it. Neighboring empires wait for Abbasiyya to weaken to take small bits of the nebula.

V
Doctrine

Haqqislam

The main religion of the haqq-caliphate is Haqqislam, a new branch of Islam that is based on Infinity’s universe. It resulted from the re-founding of this belief system from a humanistic point of view, based on the doctrine of the Search for Knowledge. This new Islam has cast off the obscurantism of times past returning to its origins, to the golden age of the great caliphates during which it was a center of knowledge. The true goal of Haqqislam is the Search for Knowledge, the belief that wisdom will guide humanity towards Complete Knowledge, and total union with Allah.
